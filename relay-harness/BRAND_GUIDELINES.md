# Relay Harness Brand Usage Guidelines

English | [中文](BRAND_GUIDELINES.zh.md)

To keep the Relay Harness ecosystem healthy, avoid user confusion, and make related resources easy to find and identify, we ask the community to follow these conventions:

- In your project's descriptive text, you may use "Relay Harness" to truthfully and accurately describe your project's relationship with Relay Harness, for example, "built on Relay Harness" or "compatible with Relay Harness". Such descriptions comply with the license and help users understand your project's positioning.
- If you wish your project name to reflect its association with the Relay Harness ecosystem, we recommend using the abbreviated "RLH" designation for naming, which is both clear and facilitates communication within the community.
- Please avoid naming your project exactly "Relay Harness" or in a way that implies it is the official distribution. Unofficial projects presented as official can confuse users and obscure which artifacts the maintainers actually publish and support.
- Please avoid using project logos or other brand materials in your promotions or presentations in a way that could cause misunderstanding, so as not to give users the false impression of official endorsement, cooperation, or authorization.

This project makes no claim to any DeepSeek trademark. "DeepSeek" appears in this repository only to name the third-party model API that the built-in LLM provider integrates with; those references describe the vendor's service and are not part of this project's brand.

We believe a clear and orderly community environment makes every developer's efforts more visible and more readily recognized. For the few cases that do not follow these conventions, we may contact the relevant parties to make appropriate adjustments in order to maintain the overall order of the ecosystem. Thank you for your understanding and support — let us work together to build a friendlier and more sustainable open-source community.
